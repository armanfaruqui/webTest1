Studio in Montreal · Shipping in North America

Désiré is an independent jewellery label making small-batch pieces in sterling silver and hand-painted enamel. From the Veevee sprout charms to the liquid-silver Ondulé ring and Lunaire earrings, every design starts as a doodle and ends as something to collect, stack and gift. Playful, a little strange, and made to be worn every day.
